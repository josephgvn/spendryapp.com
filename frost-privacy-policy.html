Frost

Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

The short version

Frost does not collect, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Photos, videos and camera frames are processed on your iPhone and never leave it.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used to find and cover faces in real time. Frames are processed in memory on your iPhone and are not recorded unless you take a photo or record a video. The microphone is turned on while the camera is in Video mode, so a recording can have sound; audio is written only while you record. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Photos and videos you choose

You pick photos and videos with Apple's photo picker, which gives Frost only the items you select. When you save a result, Frost asks for permission to add to your library; that permission does not let Frost see the rest of your library. Saved files leave out location and camera details from the original.

Data kept on your iPhone

Your settings (cover style, strength, sound and haptics) and the number of saves made today.

Recent edits: so you can pick up an edit where you left it, Frost keeps the picture or video you are editing, with its covers, in its own folder on your iPhone. These copies are not backed up and are not shared. Frost keeps the last 12 and deletes older ones. You can delete them at any time in Settings with Clear recent edits, or stop keeping them with Keep recent edits.

All of this stays on your iPhone and is deleted with the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Frost never receives your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Children

Frost is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
