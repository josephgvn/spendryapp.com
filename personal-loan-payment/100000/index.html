What is the monthly payment on a $100,000 personal loan?

At a 12% APR it is $2,224 a month over 5 years. Below is the payment and the total interest for every common term and APR.

The short answer

A $100,000 personal loan at a 12% APR costs $2,224 a month over 5 years, with $33,467 in total interest. Over 3 years the payment is $3,321 and the interest $19,572.

Where each payment goes

In the first month of a 5-year loan at a 12% APR, $1,000 of the $2,224 payment is interest and $1,224 repays the loan. The interest part gets smaller every month as the balance falls.

How to pay less for a $100,000 loan

  • Compare offers by APR and total cost, not by the monthly payment.
  • Choose the shortest term you can keep up with: 3 years instead of 5 saves $13,895 in interest.
  • Ask about origination fees; they are often taken out of the money you receive.
  • Pay extra toward the principal when you can, if the loan has no prepayment penalty.
  • Set up automatic payments; some lenders take a little off the APR for it.

Questions

What is the monthly payment on a $100,000 personal loan?

At a 12% APR it is $3,321 a month over 3 years or $2,224 over 5 years. The table above shows every term at 8%, 12%, 18% and 24%.

How much interest will I pay on a $100,000 loan?

At a 12% APR, about $19,572 over 3 years and $33,467 over 5 years.

Is a shorter or a longer term better for a $100,000 loan?

A shorter term costs less in total: 3 years instead of 5 saves $13,895 in interest at a 12% APR, but the payment is higher ($3,321 instead of $2,224).
