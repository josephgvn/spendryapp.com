Questions

Is Spendry free?

Yes, the core features are free. Premium is monthly, yearly or a one-time Lifetime purchase; subscriptions start with a one-week free trial.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Spendry never asks for a bank login. You add entries yourself, or import them from bank messages and Shortcuts automations.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. Your records are never sent to Spendry's servers, so we can't see them.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, with widgets, Siri and Shortcuts.

When will you be debt-free?

Find out in Spendry, without a bank login.
