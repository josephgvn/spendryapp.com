    Questions

    Is Spendry free?

    Yes. The core features are free. Premium is monthly, yearly or a one-time Lifetime purchase, and subscriptions start with a free week.

    Does Spendry connect to my bank?

    No. It never asks for a bank login. You add entries yourself, or import them from bank messages and Shortcuts automations.

    Where is my data stored?

    On your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. Your records are never sent to Spendry's servers, so we can't see them.

    How does the debt payoff plan work?

    Add your debts with their balances and rates. Spendry orders them with the snowball or avalanche method, adds what you can pay on top each month and shows the month you'll be debt-free.

    Can I share a budget with my partner or family?

    Yes. Shared budgets and goals sync through iCloud, and everyone uses their own Apple Account.

    Which devices does it work on?

    iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, with widgets, Siri and Shortcuts.

    Which languages and currencies does it support?

    44 languages and every major currency. Investments and foreign currency accounts can keep their own currency.
