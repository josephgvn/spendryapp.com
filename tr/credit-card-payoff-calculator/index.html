How the calculation works

Each month the calculator adds a month of interest to the balance and subtracts your payment, until the balance reaches zero. The minimum payment comparison recalculates the minimum every month from the remaining balance.

Why minimum payments take so long

The minimum falls as the balance falls, so the payment never gets ahead of the interest by much. A fixed payment keeps the same amount working on the balance every month.

Make it real

Spendry keeps the plan going: add your cards, set what you can pay each month and get a reminder before every due date.
