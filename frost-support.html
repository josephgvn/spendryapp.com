How does Frost find faces?

With Apple's Vision framework, on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, so Frost works in Airplane Mode too.

A face was not covered

Very small, turned away or motion blurred faces can be missed. In a photo, use Brush or Box and drag over the area. In a video, check each person's lane before exporting, and play the result once before you share it.

How do I keep my own face visible?

In the camera, turn on Keep me and the face closest to the camera stays visible, or tap any face. It stays visible in the photo you take. In photos and videos, tap a face and choose Visible.

Does Frost keep my photos or videos?

Only on your iPhone, for Recent edits: the last 12 edits stay in Frost's own folder so you can pick them up again. They are never uploaded or backed up. Settings in Frost has Clear recent edits to delete them and Keep recent edits to stop keeping them. Results are saved to your Photos library or shared where you choose, and location and camera details are left out of saved files.

Restore or manage a purchase

Open Settings in Frost and tap Restore purchases. To change or cancel a subscription, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
