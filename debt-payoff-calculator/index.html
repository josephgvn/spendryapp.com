Snowball or avalanche?

With the snowball method you pay off the smallest balance first. The first debt disappears quickly and its payment rolls into the next one, which keeps motivation high.

With the avalanche method you pay off the highest interest rate first. You usually pay less interest overall, though the first payoff can take longer.

The calculator runs both with the same monthly budget: every debt gets its minimum payment, the extra goes to the next debt in line, and a cleared debt's payment rolls over to the next.
