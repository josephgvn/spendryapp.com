Support

Write to support@spendryapp.com. Replies usually come within two working days.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone, in Daycamp's own storage. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. Settings › Data exports a copy of everything (JSON) or your journal (Markdown) whenever you want one.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Your Daycamp data moves with your iPhone backup: restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Quick Start. Exported files are a copy to keep; they cannot be imported back yet.

A habit does not fill in from Apple Health

On the Hero tab, open Everything else › Apple Integrations and connect Apple Health. If it is connected, open Settings on your iPhone, then Privacy & Security › Health › Daycamp, and allow the categories the habit uses.

A place automation does not run

Logging and timing at a place need location access Always: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Daycamp › Always. With While Using, Daycamp can still remind you when you arrive.

How do I change the size of a glass of water?

On Today, tap the arrow next to the water button and choose Glass Size, or open Water and change A glass is. The size you set is what one tap adds, in widgets and with Siri too.

Restore or manage a purchase

In Daycamp, open the Hero tab, then Settings › Restore Purchases. To change or cancel a subscription, use Manage Subscription there, or open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Start Fresh removes your habits, journal, tasks and progress and keeps your settings. Erase Everything removes all of it. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your iPhone.
