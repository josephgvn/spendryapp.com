Privacy Policy

Yürürlük / Effective: 24.07.2026 Sürüm / Version: 1.0 Uygulama / App: Sismy (iOS)

Sismy, deprem bilgilerini ve hissedilebilir sarsıntı uyarılarını sunan bir mobil uygulamadır. Bu politika, Sismy'yi kullandığınızda hangi kişisel verilerin işlendiğini, neden işlendiğini, ne kadar süreyle saklandığını ve bu veriler üzerindeki haklarınızı açıklar.

Özet. Sismy'de hesap yoktur. Adınızı, e-postanızı veya telefon numaranızı istemeyiz. Uygulamanın çalışması için işlediğimiz iki temel veri vardır: yaklaşık konumunuz (size yakın depremleri bildirebilmek için) ve cihazınızın bildirim kimliği (bildirimi gönderebilmek için). Verilerinizi satmayız ve reklam amacıyla üçüncü taraflarla paylaşmayız.

6.Konum Verisi Hakkında Ayrıntılı Bilgi

Konum, uygulamanın en hassas verisidir; bu nedenle nasıl işlendiğini ayrıntılı açıklıyoruz:

  • İzin türü: "Uygulamayı Kullanırken" (When In Use). Uygulama kapalıyken konumunuz okunmaz.
  • Hassasiyet: Sistemden yaklaşık 100 metre doğrulukta konum talep edilir; 500 metreden küçük hareketler yok sayılır.
  • Neden gerekli: "Size 40 km uzaklıkta M4.2 deprem" gibi bir bildirimin üretilebilmesi için sunucunun cihazın nerede olduğunu bilmesi gerekir. Karşılaştırma cihaz başına sunucuda yapılır.
  • Geçmiş tutulmaz: Sunucuda yalnızca en son bilinen konum tutulur; konum geçmişi veya hareket kaydı oluşturulmaz.
  • Kayıtlarda maskeleme: Teknik kayıtlara (log) yazılırken koordinatlar yaklaşık 11 km hassasiyete yuvarlanır.
  • İzin vermezseniz: Uygulama çalışmaya devam eder; deprem listesini ve haritayı görürsünüz. Yalnızca konuma bağlı uyarılar ve uzaklık hesabı devre dışı kalır.
  • Alternatif: Ayarlar'dan konum izni yerine elle bir şehir/konum seçebilirsiniz.

Sismy is a mobile application that provides earthquake information and possibly-felt shaking alerts. This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use Sismy, why it is processed, how long it is retained, and the rights you have over it.

Summary. Sismy has no user accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, or phone number. Two pieces of data make the app work: your approximate location (so we can alert you about nearby earthquakes) and your device push identifier (so we can deliver the alert). We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

1.Scope

This policy applies to the Sismy iOS application and the Sismy notification server it connects to. Third-party websites opened from within the app (for example EMSC or USGS) are governed by their own privacy policies; we are not responsible for their processing activities.

6.Location Data in Detail

  • Permission type: "While Using the App". Your location is not read when the app is closed.
  • Accuracy: Requested at approximately 100-metre accuracy; movements under 500 metres are ignored.
  • Why it is needed: To produce an alert such as "M4.2 earthquake 40 km from you", the server must know where the device is. The comparison is performed per device on the server.
  • No history: Only your most recent location is stored; no location history or movement profile is created.
  • Log redaction: Coordinates written to technical logs are rounded to roughly 11-kilometre precision.
  • If you decline: The app keeps working — you still see the earthquake list and map. Only location-based alerts and distance calculations are disabled.
  • Alternative: You can select a city/location manually in Settings instead of granting location access.

7.Notifications

Notifications are delivered through the Apple Push Notification service (APNs). Storing your device's APNs identifier is technically required to deliver them. Notification content (magnitude, place, distance) passes through Apple's infrastructure; see the Apple Privacy Policy.

If you delete the app, Apple invalidates your push identifier and our server automatically deletes your device record when it learns of this.

8.Advertising

The free version of Sismy displays advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may process certain device identifiers and technical information in order to serve and measure ads. This data is not collected by Sismy and is not transmitted to the Sismy server.

  • Personalised ads: If you decline on the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt, you will be shown non-personalised ads.
  • Ad-free use: A one-time in-app purchase removes advertising entirely. The transaction is handled by Apple; we have no access to your payment details.
  • Details: How Google uses data from partner sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

11.Data Security

  • All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with TLS (HTTPS).
  • The device secret is never stored in plaintext on the server — only a cryptographic hash is kept, and comparisons are protected against timing attacks.
  • On your device the secret is stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Server access is restricted and rate limiting is applied against abuse.
  • No security measure is absolute; we undertake to apply reasonable technical and organisational measures.

12.International Transfers

To deliver notifications, your data may be processed through the infrastructure of Apple (APNs) and our hosting provider. These transfers take place in the context of performing the service and on the basis of your explicit consent. Server hosting region: Frankfurt, Germany (European Union). Data is hosted within the EU, in a region covered by the GDPR.

13.Your Rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 and GDPR Arts. 15–22 you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed and request information if it is,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used accordingly,
  • Request rectification of incomplete or inaccurate data,
  • Request erasure or destruction of your data,
  • Request restriction of processing and data portability (GDPR),
  • Withdraw your consent at any time,
  • Claim compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing.

Requests can be sent to the email address in Section 15 and are answered within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or your local supervisory authority.

14.Deleting Your Data

  1. Turn notifications off — setting the scope to "Off" in Settings › Notifications deletes your server record.
  2. Delete the app — Apple invalidates your push identifier and the server clears your record automatically.
  3. Email us — write to the address in Section 15 to request deletion.

Data held on your device (downloaded earthquakes, notification history, saved items) is removed entirely when you delete the app.

15.Children's Privacy
