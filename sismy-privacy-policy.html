Bilgilerinizi satmıyoruz.

6.Konumunuz hakkında ayrıntı

Konum en hassas bilgi olduğu için nasıl kullanıldığını ayrıca açıklıyoruz:

  • Ne zaman alınır: Varsayılan olarak yalnızca uygulamayı kullanırken. Uygulama kapalıyken konumunuz okunmaz. Ayarlar bölümündeki seyahat seçeneğini kendiniz açarsanız, telefon bulunduğunuz yeri belirgin biçimde değiştirdiğinde uygulama arka planda da konumunuzu okur. Bu seçenek varsayılan olarak kapalıdır, siz açmadan sorulmaz ve istediğiniz an kapatabilirsiniz. Açıkken de sürekli takip yapılmaz, yalnızca şehir değiştirmek gibi büyük konum değişikliklerinde ölçüm alınır.
  • Ne kadar hassas: Yaklaşık 100 metre. 500 metreden küçük hareketleriniz dikkate alınmaz.
  • Neden gerekli: "Size 40 km uzaklıkta 4.2 büyüklüğünde deprem" diyebilmek için sunucunun telefonun nerede olduğunu bilmesi gerekir.
  • Geçmişiniz tutulmaz: Sunucuda yalnızca en son bilinen konumunuz durur. Nerelere gittiğinizin kaydı oluşturulmaz.
  • İzin vermezseniz: Uygulama çalışmaya devam eder, deprem listesini ve haritayı görürsünüz. Sadece konuma bağlı uyarılar ve uzaklık bilgisi çalışmaz.
  • Başka seçenek: Konum izni vermek istemiyorsanız Ayarlar'dan elle bir şehir seçebilirsiniz.

Sismy is an app that shows earthquake information and alerts you to shaking you might feel nearby. This page explains, in plain language, what information is used when you use Sismy, why, and what you can do about it.

In short: Sismy has no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email, or phone number. Two things are needed for the app to work: roughly where you are (so we can tell you about earthquakes near you) and a number that lets us send notifications to your phone. We don't sell your information.

1.What this page covers

The Sismy iPhone and Android apps, and the Sismy server they use to send notifications. If you open another website from inside the app (for example EMSC or USGS, where the earthquake data comes from), that site's own rules apply there.

6.More about your location

  • When it's taken: by default, only while you are using the app. Not when the app is closed. If you turn on the travel option in Settings yourself, the app also reads your location in the background when the phone reports a significant change of place. That option is off by default, you are not asked for it until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time. Even when it is on there is no continuous tracking, only a reading when your location changes substantially, such as moving to another city.
  • How precise: about 100 metres. Movements under 500 metres are ignored.
  • Why it's needed: to be able to say "magnitude 4.2 earthquake 40 km from you", the server has to know where the phone is.
  • No history: only your most recent location is kept on the server. No record of where you've been is created.
  • If you decline: the app still works, you see the earthquake list and the map. Only location-based alerts and distances stop working.
  • Another option: if you'd rather not grant location access, you can pick a city manually in Settings.

7.Notifications

Notifications are delivered through Apple's notification system, which is why we need to store your phone's notification address. The content of a notification (magnitude, place, distance) passes through Apple's system, see the Apple Privacy Policy for their own rules.

If you delete the app, Apple invalidates that address, and once our server learns this it deletes your record automatically.

8.Advertising

The free version of Sismy shows ads through Google AdMob. To choose ads and count how often they were shown, Google may process: your internet address (IP), your phone's advertising identifier, and general information about how you use the app. This information does not come to us, it goes directly to Google.

  • Ads matched to you: when your phone asks whether you allow this app to track you, if you decline, ads are no longer matched to your interests. You still see ads, they're just not personalised.
  • Ad-free use: a one-time purchase inside the app removes ads entirely. Payment is handled by Apple. We never see your card details.
  • Details: How Google uses data from partners · Google Privacy Policy

11.How we protect your information

  • All communication between the app and the server is encrypted (the same technology as the padlock in your browser).
  • Your phone's security code is never stored in readable form on the server, only a one-way equivalent, enough to verify but not to reverse.
  • On your phone, that code sits in iOS's protected storage.
  • Access to the server is restricted, with protection against excessive requests.
  • No security measure is perfect. We undertake to apply reasonable, up-to-date protections.

12.Where your information is processed

For notifications to reach you, your information passes through Apple's systems and our hosting provider's. The Sismy server is located in Frankfurt, Germany. İnside the European Union, in a region covered by the GDPR.

13.Your rights

KVKK and the GDPR give you the rights below. To use any of them, just write to us:

  • Find out what information of yours is used, and get a copy

14.Deleting your information

Since there are no accounts, you can do this straight from the app:

  1. Turn notifications off. Set the scope to "Off" in Settings › Notifications. Your server record is deleted.
  2. Delete the app. Your record clears itself.
  3. Write to us. Send a deletion request to privacy@spendryapp.com.

Information kept on your phone (earthquake list, notification history, saved items) is removed from the phone entirely when you delete the app.
